A practical guide to sourcing research.

Choose your starting point, set the comparison criteria and use the report to plan your next inquiry.

1. Choose your starting point

A product link

Provide a public retail product URL and the target country. Specify the variant and features that must match.

A category brief

Provide the marketplace, category, market and time window. State how many candidates you want compared.

An existing report

Provide prior findings and ask for an updated baseline or a more focused comparison. Prices and access conditions need fresh checks.

2. Run a focused comparison

Use source-scout to research this product: [PUBLIC PRODUCT URL].
Target market: [COUNTRY]. Prioritize China-based suppliers on Alibaba.
Compare up to 3 similar designs and 2 functional alternatives when public evidence supports them.
Record material, dimensions, compartments, key mechanisms, MOQ, currency and quote terms.
Separate verified product pages from search leads. Do not infer an exact match from images.
If access or specifications are missing, deliver a partial report with the gaps and search terms.
Calculate cost markup only when prices and specifications are comparable.
Create a sourcing report and a short inquiry checklist. Do not contact suppliers or place orders.

3. Read the evidence labels

Confirmed match

Use only when product identity and key specifications are established. Appearance alone does not establish factory origin or brand authorization.

Similar design or alternative

Inspect the specific differences and their effect on use. Similar styling and shared functionality are different kinds of fit.

Search lead

Treat it as a research direction. A search entry or indexed snippet is not a verified supplier recommendation.

4. Check price comparability

Cost markup = (retail unit price − sourcing unit cost) ÷ sourcing unit cost × 100%. This is not gross margin or net profit margin. Match currency, variant, unit, quantity tier and trade terms before calculating.

Freight, duties, platform fees, advertising, packaging, certification, storage, returns and capital committed to MOQ are additional costs. Missing quotes stay pending.

5. Use the deliverables

Review the HTML report, structured JSON, link-verification records and inquiry checklist. Confirm dimensions, resin, rotating mechanism, samples, order quantities and packaging before deciding whether to sample.

Public access restrictions can limit the result. Do not bypass logins or CAPTCHAs. AI-generated product illustrations are display aids, not evidence of an actual product or match.
