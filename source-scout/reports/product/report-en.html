  • The retail page was fetched again for this run: clear, single unit, USD 18.82, rated 4.8/5 from 88 ratings. These are public listing claims, not physical test results.

  • All purchase costs require a quote. Dollar-sign ranges and MOQs are listing or search-extract observations, not confirmed supplier quotes. Currency, variants, quantity tiers and trade terms remain unresolved.
  • Cost markup = (retail unit price − purchase unit cost) ÷ purchase unit cost × 100%. None is calculated: quotes cannot be matched to the baseline specification, and the lowest number in a range is not a defensible cost basis.
  • Excluded costs include freight, duties, marketplace fees, advertising, packaging, certification, storage, returns and capital committed to minimum orders. Cost markup is not gross margin or net profit margin.
  • Investigate L1 first: its title describes clear rotating storage, and the indexed 2-piece MOQ may make a small sample inquiry practical. First confirm the model, current MOQ and sample terms. This priority is not a supplier endorsement or a claim of more reliable pricing.
  • Decide whether to sample only after confirming dimensions, material, rotation and quote terms. Evaluate smooth rotation, loaded stability, edge finish and scratch protection in transit.
  • No login or access restrictions were bypassed. No supplier was contacted and no order was placed. Brand names and similar appearance do not establish factory relationships or authorization.
