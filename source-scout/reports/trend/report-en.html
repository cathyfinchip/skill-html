Risks and research limitations

  • Link validation: 2 Walmart product pages passed a public-text review. Other links did not pass the access check. Alibaba product and search paths remain unverified; the search terms below are for follow-up research only.
  • This is a public-source screening draft with 3 provisional selections from 8 candidates. It establishes neither a 30-day trend nor sales volume, bestseller rankings or procurement returns.
  • September 20, 2026 is the research date, not the source update date. Prices, ratings and availability come from differently dated index snapshots and must be rechecked for the same location and variant.
  • Cumulative ratings indicate existing customer feedback, not recent sales or growth. Walmart’s AI-generated summaries are listing descriptions, not independent verification.
  • No exact match or fully vetted supplier was established. Product-page leads, functional alternatives and search entries are labeled separately; no unsupported match percentages are assigned.
  • Cost markup = (retail unit price − purchase unit cost) ÷ purchase unit cost × 100%. No markup is calculated because currency, dimensions, unit basis or trade terms remain unresolved. Purchase costs require a quote.
  • Excluded costs include freight, duties, marketplace fees, advertising, returns, certification, packaging, storage and capital tied up in minimum orders. Cost markup is neither gross margin nor net profit margin.
  • Card images are AI-generated SVG illustrations, not product photographs or evidence of material, dimensions or a match.
  • Verification case: Lopunny’s title says 3.82 in high while its specifications say 3.93 in, and the detail page could not be read. Next, check the same variant’s specification sheet or request a dimensioned drawing. No supplier was contacted.
  • Verification sequence: same-variant retail price; supplier material, exterior and interior dimensions and construction drawing; MOQ, quote currency and trade terms; then samples and packaging. Similar appearance does not establish factory identity or brand authorization.
