DELIVERY LOGIC

Progress advances after confirmed delivery

  1. New events enter a pending-delivery state.
  2. If delivery fails or is unknown, the cursor does not advance.
  3. Confirmed delivery advances the cursor and records the event.
  4. Previously delivered events are filtered from later alerts.
This design reduces missed or duplicate alerts; it is not a guarantee of zero omissions or duplicates in every hosting environment.
PRODUCT SCOPE

What the Skill does—and does not do

Designed to

  • Discover ME-listed AI and Web3 events
  • Filter by location, category, and time
  • Organize schedules, venues, and links
  • Create and manage event subscriptions
  • Check newly listed events and send alerts

Not designed to

  • Collect or summarize industry news
  • Guarantee standardized event quality
  • Complete registration or payment
  • Guarantee organizer information never changes
  • Replace final verification on the event page
